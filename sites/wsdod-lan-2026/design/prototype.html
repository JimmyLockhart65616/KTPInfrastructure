The World Series of Day of Defeat.

Three days on the basement floor in Chinatown. A twelve-company field, 61 players, and 100 matches of Day of Defeat 1.3 — draft Friday, group play Saturday, playoffs and finals Sunday. Every match played on the KTP stack and recorded end to end.

The weekend

A Saturday 1.20 and a Sunday 1.20 are not the same number, which is why the two boards below never merge.

Rules

as enforced on the weekend · captains held the authoritative copy
Rules of engagement match rules · abridged

General conduct

1.1LAN admins may modify rules as needed for the good continuation of the tournament.
1.2Matches require the correct map, config file and LAN server. No exceptions.
1.3Server crash protocol. Down inside the first five minutes of a half: full restart. After five minutes: resume with score restored and clock rounded up. No substitutions during recovery.
1.4Pixel-walking forbidden — accessing, occupying or hanging in an area reachable only by pixel walking (e.g. harrington ladder) draws suspension and/or forfeiture.
1.5Tied matches go to two five-minute overtime halves. Failure to complete overtime is a loss for both teams.
1.7Seating assignments are final; staff may modify for operational reasons.

Cheating, penalties & disputes

2.4Prohibited tooling: visual player-location aids, recoil control, target acquisition, modified game files, null binds, monitor/software crosshairs, mouse-acceleration programs.
2.5Default game files only unless approved or provided by staff. Approved scoreboard modifiers and custom crosshairs (except sniper) allowed.
2.6Spectating after a match goes live is cheating. Spec hopping only during full-map captures or stuck-player situations.

Players & captains

3.1Pass file and cvar checks, record POV demos each half, screenshot scoreboards, remain through match conclusion.
3.3Boosting is allowed if the destination is reachable by a single player without boosting.
3.5Prohibited binds: actions coupled with attack, duck-scrollwheel macros, automated attack or movement scripts, weapon-spawn exploits.
4.1Captains submit final scores with legible scoreboard and rcon-status screenshots to the shared drive.

Pauses

5.1One two-minute pause per team per half, callable only immediately after a cap-out. Captains only, except technical crashes.
5.5Unauthorized pause: first offense awards the opposing team cap-out points; second is immediate forfeiture.
Abridged transcription. In any conflict the original wording on file at WSDoD command governs; captains were issued the authoritative copy on the day.
Code of conduct adult competitors, in good faith

Sportsmanship & respect

1.1Toxicity is common online — do better in person. Harassment, abuse or discrimination, in any form, is not tolerated.
1.2Win or lose with dignity. A little taunting is normal in this community — keep it within reason.

Fair play

2.1No cheating or exploiting of any kind. KTP rules apply wherever applicable, including the cvar checker on all LAN servers.
2.2Third-party software, scripts, exploits or tampered game files — outside approved submissions — is immediate disqualification.

During matches

3.2Matches start no later than 10 minutes after schedule regardless of who's ready. Ringers only in extenuating circumstances at staff discretion.
3.4Stream sniping is prohibited and can forfeit the match.
3.5Mumble (or in-game voice) is the only authorized comms tool.
3.7Play under the alias you signed up with. Aliasing is prohibited.

Equipment & etiquette

5.2Your equipment is your responsibility — neither the venue nor staff is liable for lost items.
5.7PC changes limited to what your peripherals need, subject to staff review — including Snap Tap. If unsure, ask first.

Penalties

7.1Warnings → match or round forfeiture → removal from the tournament → ban from future events, by severity and frequency.
Operative copy, condensed for the page. Participation is agreement; ignorance of the rules is not a defense.
